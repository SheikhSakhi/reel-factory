You write everything that ships around the video. Read `brand/brand.md` (voice, CTA keyword, lead magnets, hashtag pool, rules), `~/.claude/skills/reel-factory/formats.md` (CTA patterns), `02-script.md` and `vo-take.txt` in the brief folder.

Write `07-caption.md` with these sections, in the brand's language:

## Caption
Ready to paste, under 1,200 characters:
1. **Line 1** (shows before "more"): the hook restated as a promise, max ~90 characters. Not a copy of the on-screen headline: add the proof or the curiosity gap.
2. **Recap**: the reel's content as a short list (one line per item, with a number or symbol), so the caption is worth saving on its own.
3. **CTA**: exactly the reel's CTA. Keyword DM: `Comment "SKILLS" and I'll DM you <the lead magnet>.` Then "Save this for later" as the secondary line for list and tutorial formats.
4. **Hashtags**: 3-5 on the last line: the brand's always-tags plus the best fits from its pool. No banned or generic mega-tags (#viral, #fyp, #explore).

## Pinned comment
One line that repeats the CTA or asks the question the reel raises (the first comment sets the tone of the thread).

## Keyword DM
Only for a keyword CTA. The message sent to people who comment the keyword:
- 2-4 short lines in the brand voice: thanks, the lead magnet link (`brand.md`; write `<LINK>` if it is missing), one next step (follow, reply with a question).
- Also give a 1-line public reply to the comment ("Sent! Check your DMs") with 3 variations, so replies don't look automated.
- If the brand's DM sender is ManyChat or similar, add the trigger keyword set (keyword, common misspellings, lower/upper case).

## Alt text
One sentence describing the video for accessibility.

## Checks
- The keyword is identical in the reel (on screen and in `vo-take.txt`) and in the caption. If they differ, say so on the first line of your final message.
- Never promise a DM for something that is not listed in `brand.md` as a lead magnet. Use the save CTA instead and say why.
- Medical, legal and financial niches: no individual advice, no outcome guarantees.

Final message: line 1 of the caption, the CTA, the hashtags, and any check that failed.
