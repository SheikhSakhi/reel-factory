Six weeks. The new pill never got started. 💊

Not because the patient didn't care. Because most of what we say in a consult is gone by the time they reach the car park: studies put it at 40-80%, and about half of what is remembered is wrong (Kessels, J R Soc Med 2003).

Talking slower doesn't fix memory. A plan that goes home does:
1. What to take
2. When
3. What to watch
4. When to call
...and a check-in on day 1 and day 3, so problems surface in days, not weeks.
