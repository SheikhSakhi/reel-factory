# Facts used (one primary source)

Rotenstein LS, et al. Changes in Clinician Time Expenditure and Visit Quantity With Adoption of Artificial Intelligence-Powered Scribes: A Multisite Study. JAMA. Published online April 1, 2026; 335(16):1408-1417. https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/article-abstract/2847319

1. 8,581 ambulatory clinicians (1,809 adopters) at five US academic health systems (MGB, Emory, UCSF, Yale New Haven, UC Davis), June 2023 to August 2025.
2. Documentation time: 16.0 fewer minutes per 8 scheduled patient hours (95% CI 13.7 to 18.3).
3. After-hours EHR time: -3.1 minutes (95% CI -6.8 to 0.5), "did not change significantly".

Interpretation in the reel ("the evening is inbox, results, messages") is framed as the reason the note wasn't the whole evening; the study measured where time went, not why. Observational study; "adoption" = access.
