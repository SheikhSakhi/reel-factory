Three weeks of waiting. Two buses. And the specialist starts from zero.

Where did the referral letter go? Doctors were asked. In a US survey of 4,720 physicians (O'Malley & Reschovsky, Archives of Internal Medicine, 2011), 69% of family doctors said they send the patient's history with a referral. Only 35% of specialists said they receive it.

Both sides think they did their part. The story gets lost on the way.

A referral should travel with the question, the history and the plan. And the answer should come back to the doctor who holds the whole picture, with the next step and its date.
