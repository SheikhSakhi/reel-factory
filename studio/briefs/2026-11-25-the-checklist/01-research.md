# Research

1. Boeing Model 299 crash, Wright Field, 30 October 1935: the aircraft took off with its control (gust) locks engaged; investigators found it mechanically sound. The crash led the Army Air Corps to institutionalise the pilot's checklist; the 299 was developed into the B-17. Sources differ on the death toll and on whether the pilot had flown the 299 before, so the reel only says "an experienced army test pilot".
   - Smithsonian Air & Space Quarterly: https://www.airandspace.si.edu/air-and-space-quarterly/winter-2023/set-checked
   - Air & Space Forces Magazine, "The Checklist": https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/0813checklist/
2. Haynes AB, Weiser TG, et al. A surgical safety checklist to reduce morbidity and mortality in a global population. N Engl J Med 2009;360(5):491-9. Eight hospitals, 7,688 patients, before/after: deaths 1.5% -> 0.8%; inpatient complications 11.0% -> 7.0%. Not randomised.
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19144931/
