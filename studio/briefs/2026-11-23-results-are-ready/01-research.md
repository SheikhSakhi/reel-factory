# Research

Steitz BD, Turer RW, Lin CT, et al. Perspectives of patients about immediate access to test results through an online patient portal. JAMA Netw Open 2023;6(3):e233572. Survey of 8,139 portal users (of >43,000 invited) after the US 21st Century Cures Act made results available immediately.
- ~96% preferred immediate access, including ~95% of those with abnormal results.
- ~8% overall reported more worry after viewing; 17% among those who perceived a result as not normal (vs 5% normal).
https://news.vumc.org/2023/03/22/survey-finds-patients-value-immediate-access-to-test-results/
The reel says: "almost all wanted their results right away. But when a result looked abnormal, about one in six felt more worried after reading it." The patient story is illustrative.
