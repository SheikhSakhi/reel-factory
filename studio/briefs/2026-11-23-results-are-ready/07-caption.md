One number is red. No note. No explanation. You read it twelve times, search it, and don't sleep.

In a survey of 8,139 patients (Steitz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2023), almost all (about 96%) wanted their results right away. But among those whose result looked abnormal, about 1 in 6 (17%) felt more worried after reading it.

Fast was never the problem. Alone was. A result should arrive with a line from your doctor: what it means, whether it matters, and what happens next.

Illustrative story. AI voice. Not medical advice.
