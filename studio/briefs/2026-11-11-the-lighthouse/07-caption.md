Patients are the same. Most don't call until it's a storm. But the small changes come first: with heart failure, a study of home daily weights (Chaudhry et al., Circulation, 2007) found weight starts creeping up at least a week before people end up in hospital.

A visit every three months can't see that week. A light between the visits can: a scale every morning, a line agreed together, and someone who sees when it's crossed.
