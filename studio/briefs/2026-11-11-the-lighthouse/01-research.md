# Research

Chaudhry SI, Wang Y, Concato J, Gill TM, Krumholz HM. Patterns of weight change preceding hospitalization for heart failure. Circulation 2007;116:1549-1554. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2892745
- Nested case-control study, 134 patients hospitalized for heart failure vs 134 matched controls, in a home daily-weight telemonitoring programme.
- Conclusion: increases in body weight are associated with hospitalization for heart failure and begin at least 1 week before admission (weight drifted up over ~30 days; the groups diverged in the final week).
The reel says: "with heart failure, weight. A study found it starts creeping up at least a week before people end up in hospital." No thresholds or advice are given. Characters are illustrative.
