# Caption

Send sepsis survivors home with a monitor, and fewer of them come back. That's the logic.

A trial just tested it. 1,286 patients after sepsis or pneumonia. Vitals and symptoms tracked from home. Four monitoring strategies.

90-day readmissions: 36.3-44.2% with monitoring, 37.8% with usual follow-up. No significant difference. Older patients were readmitted more often, not less.

A monitor collects numbers. Someone still has to decide what each number means, for this patient. More data isn't more care. A plan is.

Source: ACCOMPLISH trial, Yende et al., JAMA Network Open, June 2026 (UPMC). Usual care in this trial included nurse follow-up.
