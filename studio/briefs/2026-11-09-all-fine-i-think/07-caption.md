"Just left the doctor. All fine, I think."

"You think?"

He was there. She explained it clearly. By the car park, it was gone.

That's normal. A review of patients' memory (Kessels, J R Soc Med, 2003) found that 40 to 80 percent of what doctors say is forgotten almost straight away, and almost half of what is remembered is wrong.

The fix isn't a better memory. It's the plan, written down, from the same doctor: what changed, how to take it, what to check, the next visit, and one place to ask. Written information is better remembered.
