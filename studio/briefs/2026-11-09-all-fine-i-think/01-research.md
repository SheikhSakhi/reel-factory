# Research

Kessels RPC. Patients' memory for medical information. J R Soc Med 2003;96(5):219-222. doi:10.1258/jrsm.96.5.219. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC539473/ (narrative review)
1. "40-80% of medical information provided by healthcare practitioners is forgotten immediately."
2. "almost half of the information that is remembered is incorrect" (cited to one rheumatology clinic study, Anderson et al. 1979).
3. "Written information is better remembered and leads to better treatment adherence." Conclusion: spoken information should be supported with written or visual material.
