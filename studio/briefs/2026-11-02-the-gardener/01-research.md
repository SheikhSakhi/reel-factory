# Research

1. **A year has 8,760 hours** (365 × 24). Arithmetic.
2. **"Maybe an hour a year"**: spoken by the character as his estimate (e.g. four 15-minute follow-ups = 60 minutes). It's a character's line, not a statistic; the reel doesn't present it as data.
3. The reframe ("grown on the days you're not there") is an opinion and a metaphor, not a claim.

No other facts are used. No medical advice: the practical line ("ask one question a week, notice who wilts first") is about how a clinician pays attention between visits, not about treatment.
