## Score

- A slow minor-ninth pad (D) that breathes with the acts; harmony shifts at act boundaries.
- Ducked by about 7 dB under speech (envelope from word timings, 120 ms attack, 400 ms release).
- Act textures:
  - **expert**: soft mechanical relay clicks on each rule that fires; no pulse.
  - **ml / training**: rhythmic muted pulses that tighten as the error shrinks; stop dead at "Once trained".
  - **infer**: near-silence, a single sustained tone as the patient passes.
  - **dl**: a low tone drops a step at each layer the camera passes (depth change).
  - **gen**: light, airy shimmer under the draft composing.
  - **compute**: broad granular texture built from many short tones firing together (parallelism you can hear).
  - **end**: pad resolves to a single open fifth, then silence under the last line.
