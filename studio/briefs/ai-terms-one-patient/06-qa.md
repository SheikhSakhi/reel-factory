## Fix list (most severe first)

1. **caution · B41 · f8450–8500 (worst at f8469): double exposure makes the frame unreadable.** The "trained on past patient records / used on new patients" panel and the draft-document panel fade over the same ~30 frames, so both sets of serif text overprint. The screen-space "source record" label (top 262) also lands on the TREATMENT card while the camera is still moving. **Fix:** make the crossfade sequential: `firstHalf` reaches 0 before `doc` starts (end `firstHalf` at `at("B41","and")-4` and start `doc` at `at("B41","and")+2`). Anchor "source record" to the source-card block in world space (AtPoint above `srcBase`) instead of a fixed `top: 262`.

2. **caution · B41 · f8580–8620: the key caution cue is illegible on a phone.** "not found in record · check" renders at about 16–18 px, the "output" tag at about 16 px, and the source-card keys at about 14 px. This sentence carries the whole B41 point ("doesn't guarantee that every sentence matches the record"). **Fix:** raise the on-plane size so it renders at ≥ 28 px effective (about `monoLabel(54)` at the current plane scale), or move it into a screen-space callout next to the dashed bar. Also raise "output" and the source keys to ≥ 24 px effective.

3. **dl · B19 · f4029–4154: layer 4 reads like a diagnostic heatmap, and the disclaimer vanishes.** The camera flies into layer 4, where a bright, localized cyan block cluster sits over the upper-right zone of the radiograph. Clinicians will read that as a saliency map ("the model found something here"), exactly while "help identify a finding" is spoken. At the same moment the "conceptual illustration · not a real model's features" line (22 px, muted) sits on a bright cyan background and is unreadable (f4060, f4140). f4098–4154 is screen-filling flat blocks with no readable content. "LAYER 4 · COMPLEX PATTERNS" lands at y≈1670–1780, outside the safe zone. **Fix:** make the layer-4 activation spatially uniform or abstract (no single bright region tied to anatomy), and stop the camera before it enters layer 4 so the stack stays legible. Give the disclaimer a dark backing plate and size ≥ 26 px. Keep layer labels inside y ≤ 1500.

4. **open · B03 · f450–520 (f482): the opening stacks the four terms as four equal levels.** AI / ML / DL / GenAI are four same-size slabs locked into one vertical tower. That is exactly the "four stages" reading the fidelity guard forbids, and it is the first structural image the viewer gets. **Fix:** lock the slabs nested (each smaller slab inside the previous one's footprint), and offset the GenAI slab so it straddles the DL slab and extends outside it. Or keep the planes separate and only link them, deferring structure to the relate act.

5. **relate · B33–B34 · f6629–6939: the "generative AI" label is outside the safe zone.** The label and its sub-line sit at y≈1570–1730 with the sub-line running to x≈1040, under typical platform UI and caption bars. This is the single most important relationship label in the film. **Fix:** move `genLabel` so it lands inside y ≤ 1450 and x ≤ 1000 (for example the right side of the AI ring at mid-height, with the leader line to the dashed circle), or pull the camera back slightly in the relate pose.

## Overall readability (item 7)

The explanation largely works without reading every label. The arc rules → records → train/infer → pixels → layers → draft → foundation → nested map is visually coherent, and the expert-system, ML and relate acts read at a glance. Two places break comprehension:

- The DL layer-4 fly-through (item 3) is both unclear and medically misleading.
- The caution act's crossfade and tiny "check" cue (items 1–2) hide the film's main safety message.

The opening 4-slab tower (item 4) also teaches the wrong structure before the relate act corrects it.
