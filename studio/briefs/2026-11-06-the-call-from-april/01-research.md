# Research

1. Roy CL, Poon EG, Karson AS, et al. Patient safety concerns arising from test results that return after hospital discharge. Ann Intern Med 2005;143(2):121-128. 2,644 patients discharged from two Boston academic hospitals (2004): about 41% left with at least one lab or imaging result pending; about 9% of those pending results were judged potentially actionable; physicians were unaware of many actionable results (secondary sources report "almost two-thirds"; the reel says "many" because the exact figure wasn't checked against the paper).
   - PSNet summary: https://psnet.ahrq.gov/issue/patient-safety-concerns-arising-test-results-return-after-hospital-discharge
   - Today's Hospitalist summary: https://todayshospitalist.com/The-test-results-are-in-but-the-patient-has-gone-home-Will-anyone-notice/
2. Pereira Gray DJ, Sidaway-Lee K, White E, Thorne A, Evans PH. Continuity of care with doctors: a matter of life and death? A systematic review of continuity of care and mortality. BMJ Open 2018;8:e021161. 22 observational studies; 18 found higher continuity of care with doctors associated with lower mortality (generalists and specialists). Observational only, no pooled effect size. https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/8/6/e021161
