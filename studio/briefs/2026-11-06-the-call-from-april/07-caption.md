"The hospital just called. About a scan from April."

It's September.

The result came back after she went home. The doctor who ordered it had moved to another team. It fell between teams.

This isn't rare. In one study of 2,600 patients leaving two Boston hospitals (Roy et al., Annals of Internal Medicine, 2005), about 4 in 10 went home with test results still pending, and their doctors were unaware of many of the results that needed action.

Before you leave hospital, it's fair to ask: who will call me about the results?
