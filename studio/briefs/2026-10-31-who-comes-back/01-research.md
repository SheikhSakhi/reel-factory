# Facts used

1. Jencks SF, Williams MV, Coleman EA. Rehospitalizations among patients in the Medicare fee-for-service program. N Engl J Med 2009;360:1418-28. https://www.commonwealthfund.org/publications/journal-article/2009/apr/rehospitalizations-among-patients-medicare-fee-service
   - 19.6% of 11,855,702 Medicare beneficiaries discharged from hospital were rehospitalized within 30 days (claims 2003-2004).
   - For medical discharges to the community, about half (50.2%) of those rehospitalized within 30 days had no physician office visit billed in between.
2. van Walraven C, et al. Derivation and validation of an index to predict early death or unplanned readmission after discharge from hospital to the community. CMAJ 2010;182(6):551-7. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2845681/
   - LACE: Length of stay, Acuity of admission, Comorbidity (Charlson), Emergency department visits in the previous 6 months.
   - Scores 0 to 19 = 2.0% to 43.7% expected risk of death or urgent readmission within 30 days (derivation cohort). C statistic 0.684 (moderate).

The script says "almost one in five", "about two percent ... forty four" and "about half"; it does not give a high-risk cut-off (none is validated in the sources).
