# Caption

Before a patient leaves, the hospital can already estimate whether they'll be back.

In one large US study, 19.6% of Medicare patients were back in hospital within 30 days. So doctors built a score: LACE. Length of stay, Acuity, Comorbidities, Emergency visits. Zero points: about 2% risk of coming back or dying within a month. Nineteen: about 44%.

But in that same study, about half of the medical patients who came back had seen no doctor in between.

The score said who was at risk. Nobody called them on day three.

Sources: Jencks et al., NEJM 2009; van Walraven et al., CMAJ 2010. Imagery: NASA. The city, hospital and example patient are illustrations.
