# Brand

This is the starter brand. Run `/reel-brand` to replace it with yours; every agent reads this file, and `src/brand/brand.ts` holds the matching code.

## Account
- **Name:** Starter
- **Handle:** @yourhandle
- **Niche:** (one line: who you help with what, e.g. "AI workflows for doctors")
- **Audience:** (who stops scrolling, and what they want)
- **Language:** English
- **Proof points:** (true, specific credibility the hook can use: results, credentials, numbers. Never invented.)

## Voice
- **Tone:** direct, warm, specific. No hype words.
- **Voice-over:** en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural, +6%
- **Words to avoid:** (e.g. "game-changer", "revolutionary")

## Look (locked)
- **Palette:** bg #F3EEE4 · fg #171512 · muted #6B6459 · accent #BF3F0A · surface #FFFFFF
- **Alt (contrast scenes):** bg #171512 · fg #F3EEE4
- **Fonts:** display Instrument Serif · body Inter · mono JetBrains Mono
- **Highlight:** one accented word per headline, italic in the accent colour
- **Chrome:** counter "01 / 08", label "STEP n OF N", chapter tabs, progress bar
- **Captions:** pill, word by word, band y 1340-1500

## Calls to action
- **Default keyword:** GUIDE
- **Lead magnets** (what a keyword DM delivers; only list things that exist):
  - GUIDE: the full guide (link: …)
- **DM sender:** manual (or ManyChat / another tool)

## Hashtags
- Always: (2-3 niche tags)
- Pool: (10-15 more; the caption writer picks 3-5)

## Rules
- Educational only. No individual medical, legal or financial advice in reels, captions or replies.
